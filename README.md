## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **METAL GEAR SOLID 4: Guns of the Patriots - Master Collection Version**. This trainer enhances the single-player campaign by modifying health, ammunition, weapon handling, stealth systems, Drebin Points, and selected survival mechanics. It is designed around the game's real systems, including OctoCamo, Solid Eye, CQC, weapon customization, rations, Psyche, Stress, and Drebin's weapon economy.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents Snake's health from decreasing during combat and environmental hazards.
- **Unlimited Psyche** — Keeps the Psyche meter from falling, helping Snake maintain accuracy and combat effectiveness.
- **Unlimited Stress Control** — Reduces the impact of battlefield Stress on Snake's performance.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Ammo** — Provides unlimited ammunition for equipped firearms and compatible weapons.
- **No Reload** — Removes reload delays while using supported firearms.
- **Unlimited Suppressor Durability** — Prevents equipped suppressors from wearing out during extended firefights.
- **Perfect Accuracy** — Improves weapon precision while aiming through the game's third-person and first-person targeting systems.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Maximum Drebin Points** — Sets Drebin Points to a high value for purchasing weapons, ammunition, suppressors, and upgrades from Drebin's shop.
- **Unlimited Rations and Items** — Keeps supported consumable items available for repeated use.

### 📌 Stealth

- **No Alert** — Prevents standard enemy alert escalation during infiltration.
- **Instant OctoCamo Adaptation** — Maintains rapid camouflage adaptation when Snake presses against compatible surfaces.
- **Freeze Enemy Position** — Stops selected enemy movement for easier observation, positioning, or silent takedowns.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move normally through open combat zones, urban battlefields, jungles, interiors, and other mission areas. OctoCamo adapts Snake's appearance to nearby surfaces when he presses against them, while the trainer's stealth options can reduce the consequences of movement and exposure. Disable enemy-freezing or alert protection when you want the original infiltration challenge.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the weapon and item menus to equip firearms, explosives, rations, the Solid Eye, and other mission equipment. Unlimited ammunition and item options support extended testing without repeated resupply. Drebin Points can be increased to purchase weapons, ammunition, suppressors, and customization parts from Drebin's mobile shop.

### World & Environment

Explore the campaign's varied environments and scripted mission spaces while using the trainer to experiment with alternate routes and equipment. The trainer is intended for campaign progression and replay sessions; it does not add new maps, missions, story content, or multiplayer functionality.

### Combat & Weapons

Use CQC for close-quarters takedowns, disarms, throws, and interrogations, or engage enemies with Snake's wide selection of firearms and explosives. Unlimited health, Psyche, ammunition, suppressors, and accuracy options make it easier to practice boss encounters, test weapon builds, and replay large-scale battles.
